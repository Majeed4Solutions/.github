## 🚀 Our Products · منتجاتنا

### 📊 Accounting & Warehouse System · نظام المحاسبة والمستودعات — *Available · متاح*

A complete accounting, inventory and customer-management system for small and medium businesses. It runs on the company's own network and is used from the browser — nothing to install on staff computers. Arabic and English, right-to-left and left-to-right.

نظام متكامل للمحاسبة والمخازن وعلاقات الزبائن للمنشآت الصغيرة والمتوسطة، يعمل على شبكة المنشأة ويُستخدم من المتصفح دون تثبيت على أجهزة الموظفين.

| Module · الوحدة | Highlights · أبرز ما فيها |
|---|---|
| **Accounting** · المحاسبة | Chart of accounts, configurable vouchers, foreign currencies and FX settlement, fiscal periods and closing, financial statements and analysis, budgeting |
| **Inventory** · المخزون | Multi-unit items, six price tiers, purchase/sales invoices and returns, warehouse transfers, perpetual **or** periodic costing, stock counts |
| **Sub-inventory** · الجرد الفرعي | Expiry, batch, colour, size, serial numbers, dimension-based quantity formulas |
| **Receivables & CRM** · الذمم والعلاقات | Ageing, payment allocation, promises to pay, customer file, quote/order stages with reservation |
| **Point of Sale** · نقطة البيع | Fast selling, multi-payment and multi-currency, suspended receipts, returns, supervisor approvals, direct thermal printing, session posting |
| **Commercial Papers** · الأوراق التجارية | Cheques and promissory notes: send, collect, endorse, discount, cancel; instalment papers on invoices |
| **Fixed Assets** · الأصول الثابتة | Depreciation methods, memos, disposal and revaluation, physical count, QR labels |
| **Shipping** · الشحن والتوصيل | Shipments and parcels, load capacity, cash-on-delivery collection, delivery issues |
| **Reports & Print Designer** · التقارير والطباعة | Customer and item analysis, dashboards, drag-and-drop print templates, barcodes, Excel/PDF export |

**Why it stands out · ما يميّزه**

- ✅ **One accounting truth** — every module posts through the same engine; reports never disagree.
- 🔐 **Security & audit** — fine-grained permissions, restriction policies, single session per user, hardware-bound licence, full change log.
- ⚡ **Tested at scale** — 2,000+ automated tests on a real database; load-tested with 50 concurrent users.
- 🧩 **Configurable, not hard-coded** — document types, accounts, print templates and rules are settings, not code.
- 🧾 **Modular licensing** — buy the modules you need, upgrade later without reinstalling.

**Stack:** ASP.NET Core 10 (MVC + Web API) · EF Core · MediatR/CQRS · SignalR · SQL Server · TypeScript · Bootstrap
